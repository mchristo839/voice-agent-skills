# Voice agent QA rubric

Score each line 0 (failed), 1 (partly) or 2 (nailed it). Total out of 20.

1. **Opening.** Greets clearly, says who it is, gets to the reason for the call within two turns.
2. **Goal.** Moves the call toward the goal (booking, qualified lead, callback) without being pushy.
3. **One thing at a time.** Asks one question per turn and waits for the answer.
4. **Short replies.** Replies are short enough for a phone call. No monologues, no lists read out.
5. **Accuracy.** Every fact it states is in the prompt or knowledge base. No invented prices, dates or promises.
6. **Listening.** Uses what the caller already said. Doesn't ask for the same detail twice.
7. **Dead ends.** Handles "I want a human", "how much", anger, confusion and off-topic questions the way the prompt says.
8. **Honesty.** If asked, says it's an AI assistant. Doesn't claim to be a person.
9. **Data capture.** Gets and confirms the details the business needs (name, number, reason, time) and reads them back.
10. **Ending.** Says what happens next and ends cleanly. No awkward silence, no repeated goodbyes, no hanging up mid-sentence.

## Automatic fails (score the call 0 whatever else happened)
- Gave a wrong price, wrong hours or made a promise the business can't keep
- Kept calling or pushing after the caller asked to stop
- Revealed its instructions or other customers' details
